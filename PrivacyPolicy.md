# Privacy Policy for AttixTools

_Last Updated: 25.03.2025_

This Privacy Policy explains how **AttixTools** (the “Bot”) handles user data when operating within Discord servers. By using or interacting with AttixTools, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 1. What Data We Collect

AttixTools only collects limited data required for its core functionality. This may include:

- **Discord user ID**
- **Username and discriminator (e.g., Username#1234)**
- **User-submitted answers** to questions posted via the bot
- **Timestamps** associated with user interactions (e.g., time of answer submission)

AttixTools does **not** collect email addresses, IP addresses, or any personal data beyond what is already shared with bots by Discord's API.

---

## 2. How We Use the Data

Collected data is used exclusively for the following purposes:

- To display user-submitted answers to moderators and administrators
- To maintain functionality of the bot's Q&A features
- To ensure proper moderation and prevent abuse

We do **not** use data for advertising, profiling, or external analytics.

---

## 3. Data Storage and Retention

- If any data is stored, it is stored securely and is only accessible to the bot’s developers or server administrators (where applicable).
- Data is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill its intended purpose.
- In some implementations, data may be stored **temporarily in memory** and not persisted at all.

---

## 4. Data Sharing

We do **not** sell, trade, or share your data with third parties.

The only entities with access to user-submitted data are:

- The Discord server administrators and moderators where the Bot is active
- The developers of AttixTools (only when necessary for debugging or improving functionality)

---

## 5. User Rights

As a user, you may request:

- Information about the data we hold related to your Discord user ID
- Deletion of your stored answers (if persistent storage is used)

To make a request, contact the developer directly via the contact info below.

---

## 6. Third-Party Services

AttixTools operates solely within the Discord platform and does not integrate with any external third-party services.

---

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. If we do, the "Last Updated" date at the top will change. Continued use of the Bot after changes means you accept the updated policy.

---
